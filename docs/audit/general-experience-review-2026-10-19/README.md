# General gameplay review — October 19, 2026

This pass covers the first four story jobs, ordinary movement and vehicle interaction, driving/combat regressions, and native graphics/HUD inspection. Work stays on `codex-reviews`; no asset geometry or downloaded audio changed in this pass.

## Changes

- Warehouse shutter: use whole vehicle footprints and lookahead instead of only corners. Ordinary dispatched police/SWAT yield like the scripted pursuers, so they cannot indefinitely prevent the cargo bay from sealing. Player, inactive and uncrewed vehicles still block the door safely.
- Stable handwork: grounded footing now gates digging, lockpicking, bag retrieval, uniform changes, ID inspection and records entry. Dig progress survives interruption; lockpick progress is retained for 10 seconds after the last work; interrupted uniform progress decays at 0.5 seconds per second. An interrupted ID check restarts from the beginning. The existing 7.4-second records search runs as an indoor scene after valid entry.
- Summit cargo recovery: from stable footing within reach of a stopped, intact carrier's rear at the same elevation, retrieve the package into the backpack. The player can continue on foot or use another car after a carrier becomes stranded. Unrecovered cargo still fails when destroyed.
- Visible backpack: the existing fitted torso pack follows the player when carrying the summit package on foot, hides in a seat or under a parachute, and clears when cargo is stowed or delivered.
- Readable HUD: desktop subtitles in street/chase view clear the measured minimap/card/control hints/equipment, including short windows. Long location names wrap before the navigation pill, and the gate greeting suits any time of day. An action prompt docks early when dialogue needs its position, remains docked for that action, and clears the desktop terrain-status chip.
- NPC dodges: integrate the original fading leap speed over the actual elapsed interval. A coarse off-camera update previously shortened the leap or omitted it completely; visible and coarse steps now reach the same intended distance. Panic choices, directions, traits, peak speed and collision substeps are retained. This does not add simultaneous continuous vehicle/person time-of-impact integration.
- Console trail pilot: project onto the graded route and plan corners/braking in physical distance. This repairs the automated test/console driver; ordinary player vehicle controls, the challenge timer and downhill controller remain unchanged.
- Two test fixtures were repaired to follow the actual respawn closure and newly added reset dependency. Their assertions were retained.

## Boundaries

This is a measured bug-fix and polish pass, not a claim of commercial AAA production parity or the absence of every possible bug. Automated route planners can make bad turns; those attempts are kept separate from confirmed production defects. Full ordinary objective journeys, later narrow HUD/helper regressions and staged visual inspection have separate source versions and receipts. In particular, staged summit/fort images do not prove ordinary mission completion, and later presentation or console-pilot checks do not retroactively move the recorded wins onto a newer build.
