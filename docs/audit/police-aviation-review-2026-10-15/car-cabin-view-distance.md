# Street-view cabin visibility

The orthographic camera moves backwards to put its near plane above the tallest roof and cloud-shadow plane. Its framing stays the same, but occupant culling and glass tint incorrectly used the longer physical-camera distance. Even at close inspection zoom 11, the old 37 m occupant check rejected the cabin and the glass had already reached its 0.92 distant tint.

`updateCarViewDistance()` now runs once after the final camera matrix update, including chase/ride overrides, camera kick and pixel alignment. Street view uses the original eye at `Math.hypot(680, 560) / worldZoom` along its actual viewing direction. A reused vector supplies occupant distance; one shared scalar uniform removes the identical setback from the glass shader's view-space vector. Inspection yaw, pitch and lift are retained. Every perspective camera uses its physical eye and resets the shared offset to zero immediately. The physical camera, projection, clipping, glass fade range (24–36 m), occupant reach (37 m), nearest-16 budget and player priority are unchanged.

`node tools/tests/car-cabin-view-distance.mjs` passed 165 host assertions using production camera setup, occupant queue and glass patch with real Three.js matrices. It checks two roof ceilings, three zooms, three inspection configurations, off-axis distance agreement, final-camera translation, fade/reach bounds, queue replacement/player priority and transitions to flight/chase/ride cameras with an already-compiled pane. This evaluates the shader's distance term on the CPU; it does not compile GLSL or draw occupants.
