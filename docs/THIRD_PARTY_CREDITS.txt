RENDERING TECHNIQUES
Written for this project in src/postfx3d.js, src/lighting3d.js and src/surfaces3d.js; no
third-party shader source is copied. The techniques follow published work:
- Scalable Ambient Obscurance — Morgan McGuire, Michael Mara, David Luebke (HPG 2012).
- Bloom downsample/upsample filters — Jorge Jimenez, "Next Generation Post Processing in
  Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare" (SIGGRAPH 2014).
- FXAA — Timothy Lottes (NVIDIA), fast approximate anti-aliasing.
- The ACES filmic fit is the one three.js r160 uses (MIT, vendor/three.r160.js); its inverse
  in city_hdr_pars is derived from it.
