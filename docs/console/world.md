# Console: world

`addConsoleMethods('world', …)` in `src/game-console-world.js`. World and places: map probes, the plan as data, terrain and towns, weather, airfields, rooftops, the drawbridge, GPS, Monarch Isle, North Point Key.

| Method | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `sky(id)` | Force the weather (`clear`, `fair`, `cloudy`, `overcast`, `rain`, `storm`); no argument hands the sky back to the weather machine |
| `places()` | Named businesses and landmarks with coordinates, their `kind` and the `door` the action key opens |
| `wetness(value)` | Set how wet the streets are (0 dry .. 1 soaked); with the rain stopped they dry on from there, so a test can look at a drying street at once; returns `weather()` |
| `weather()`, `weatherFront(seconds)`, `lightning(distance)` | The weather machine's state (sky, next step, rain, wet, wind, `approach`, showers, strikes, thunder pending); bring a shower in after `seconds` (overcast now, the build-up, then rain; unlocks the sky); a lightning strike `distance` map units from the player (returns where, and when its thunder arrives) |
| `probe(x, y, r)` | What occupies a map point: land/water, solid, road, rail, beach, whether a car or a jet ski fits |
| `mountainRoad(name, step, [x, y])` | The scenic mountain roads (terrain-roads.js): per road width, length, points, samples, whether graded, tightest curve (m, where), top height, steepest grade %, sharpest vertical curve (m, where), largest bank %, how far the ground on the lanes strays from the designed surface (m, where), the worst bump as felt at 100 km/h (g; open road and junction mouths), metres of road beside a drop steeper than the fall grade (`dropFaceM`, steepest side grade, where), rails / walls / rail metres / reflector posts; the viewpoints (road, point, height, drop) and totals. With a road's name only that road and its profile every `step` samples (`[x, y, height, fall left %, fall right %, relief, pinned height]`); with a map point too, its right lane's ground and designed surface every 2 units round it |
| `mountainRoadDrive(name, kmh, from, seconds, reverse, type)` | Put the player in a car (`supercar` by default) on a scenic road at `from` (share of its length) in the right-hand lane and drive it on an autopilot (steers to a point ahead, holds `kmh` but no more than ~0.75 g of cornering): distance, time, average and top speed, how far it strayed from the lane (m), steps off the tarmac, the largest vertical acceleration from the ground (g, where), height step per frame, knocks (and what was hit: `knockWith`), hops, damage, NaN. Clears other vehicles off that road first (Monarch Isle's cars and the car the player leaves too) |
| `treeAudit()` | Trees of the plan standing where a tree may not (county-build.js planTreeProblem: carriageway, building, under a rail deck, runway, doorway) with positions, and how many `prunePlanTrees` took out at build (`pruned`) |
| `terrain()` | The Ridgeline Range: each height field's grid, highest point and build time per stage (ms), the two summits' heights, each 4x4 trail's length, summit, steepest graded pitch and trailhead height, forest / boulder / stream counts, and each rock outcrop's ground height and clearance from the trails |
| `mountainTowns()` | The mountain villages (mountain-village.js): each town's buildings by kind, businesses (footprint, eaves and ridge in metres, door, place), the street dressing counts, the rescue helipad, the club block, buildings skipped for a road, and with WebGL the meshes and triangles per town (`render`) |
| `airfields()` | The runways (airfields.js): designations, length, width and blast pads in metres, thresholds, aiming point and touchdown zone, approach lights, what each PAPI shows the player's aircraft (`'RRWW'`, nearest the runway first; on-slope with nobody flying), the runway piers, the taxiway count and every plane (airframe, position, district, hp) |
| `route(x, y)` | Set a map waypoint and report the GPS route from the player: status, road length, the bridges it crosses |
| `drawbridge(action, degrees)`, `drawbridgeLook(spot, zoom)`, `drawbridgeTraffic(count)` | The Palm Sound drawbridge: `'status'`, `'open'` (an opening now: horn, bells, arms, locks, a minute's rise, the brigantine, the lowering), `'close'`, `'hold'` with degrees (arms down, leaves held there), `'snap'` with degrees (leaves there at once, for screenshots); the report has the phase, angle, gap, tip height, leaf length, clear width at the ship's mastheads, lock bars, camera factor, onlookers, traffic on the causeway, the ship (leg, sails), jumps and the layout (trunnions, gates, stop lines). Stand at a viewpoint (`'channel'`, `'west'`, `'east'`, `'north'`, `'south'`, `'tower'`, `'overview'`, `'pit'`); put `count` cars in the lanes into the bridge on each approach |
| `layout()` | The plan as data (coast, streets, rail, buildings, helipads, ships, props, foot obstacles, street ends, crosswalks, doors, colliders) for overlap audits |
| `blueHourEntrance()` | The Blue Hour hotel's street entrance (roofmission-entrance.js): `door`, the kept-clear `forecourt` rect, the hotel's `limos` (x, y, heading, colour, `driven`), the doormen and valet on post (`staff`), street `props` and `busStops` on the forecourt (both should be empty) and `walkBlocked` (the walk along the pavement past the door: should be false) |
| `rooftops(x, y)` | Rooftop helipads, the roof the player stands on, the roof under the player's helicopter (floor, clearance); with a map point, that building's roof: height, whether it is landable, archetype and roof plant (`roofKeepOuts`) |
| `bailOut(metres, x, y, heading)`, `openParachute()`, `parachuteState()`, `parachuteFallTo(target)`, `parachuteView()` | Falls group (falls-console.js): jump from a fresh helicopter `metres` above the ground at (x, y) (default: here); pull the ripcord as a fresh press of the bail key would (freefall only: a second press never reopens); the parachute's stage, `armed` (the bail key let go since the jump), deployment `phase` (pilot, lines, snivel, snap, open) and `deployS` since the pull, `opening` (canopy inflation), height above ground (`aglM`; `altitudeM` above the datum), descent rate, `loadG` / `peakLoadG` (the opening shock), where it was pulled (`openedAtM`, `pullMs`), fully open (`openAtM`, `openTimeS`, `openLostM`: altitude lost from the pull), down to the landable 7 m/s the forecast counts to (`safeLostM`, `safeTimeS`), `brakesSet` (still on the deployment brakes) and `flownS` (seconds flying) and the freefall cue's state, the height opening needs (`needM`) and its `seconds`; `parachuteFallTo` steps the jump at 30 Hz until `target` metres above the ground, or a cue state (`'soon'`, `'now'`, `'danger'`, `'short'`) or phase (`'snivel'`, `'open'`...) by name, then reports (`landed` once down). Hold the simulation (`holdSimulation`) around a scripted jump: the live frame loop otherwise arms and opens it between calls. `parachuteView()` (WebGL only, else null): the rig as drawn last frame: phase, crown height and span (m), pressurised share `fill`, `bunch`, `flog`, slider height and run, line mode, bag, pilot chute, `upright`, pendulum `pitchDeg`, deployment `brakes`, `accelG`, the in-cloud light and amount, `finite` |
| `fallState()`, `cliffSpot(kind)`, `fallTest(kind, arg)` | The player's fall (altitude, ground, falling / tumbling, the splat, the vehicle's airborne / overturned state) with the last impacts and vehicle landings; a cliff to test on (`'lethal'`, `'car'`: lip, heading, eight-way keys, heights, predicted landing, the ground profile off it); scripted runs through the real step: `'drop'` (`arg` metres onto flat ground), `'walk-cliff'`, `'walk-trail'` (down Mount Ascent), `'drive-cliff'` (a sedan off the biggest drop), `'drive-trail'` (a 4x4 down the trail), `'drive-road'` (a sedan down the county road with the biggest climb, or `error` if none climbs); `arg` is otherwise seconds |
| `monarch()` | Monarch Isle: coast, 100 m grid, streets and roundabouts, villas, towers (`reserveTowers`: designs kept but not built), `monarchOne` (the tower's storeys, roof and mast-top metres, helipad; the gate arm's lift and whether its vehicle body is up; canopy, turning circle, pool, cove, jetty, the tender, staff on post, walls), businesses, marina; traffic (`traffic`, `trafficMoving`, `trafficWaiting`, `trafficStuck`: still over 30 s, `stopped` with what holds each car), walkers (`people`, `staff`, `roles`, `onRoad`), `walkOnRoad` (walk links that run along a carriageway: should be empty), boats |
| `regencyTraffic(seconds)` | Monarch Isle's Regency Road traffic, measured (monarch-life-traffic.js): clears the road's island cars, puts three on its lanes (up from the bridge, down from the top, one about to turn round), leaves the player on foot beside it and steps the world `seconds` (60). Per car: `wrongSideM` (furthest its middle got over the centre line in the bends, negative = right of it), `eaglePassSteps` / `eaglePassGapM` (steps with a corner in Eagle Pass's carriageway, closest it came), `offRoad`, `turns` (turn-rounds at the road's end), `longestStopS`, damage |
| `skyline()`, `skylineVisit(spot)` | North Point Key (skyline-console.js): the three towers (lot, height m, roof use, pad, deck), the 15 in reserve, the islet (land, district, street end, bridge water/channels, palms, solids), the lifts (lobby and roof doors, floors), the helicopter on the pad (`onPad`), CIRRUS (`live`, `people`, `seatsOnDeck`, who is `talking`, the `lines` being said), the offices on the old cluster blocks (retired towers, buildings built, tallest m) and the player (`roof`, `onDeck`, altitude, lift ride). `skylineVisit` stands the player at `'bridge'`, `'gate'`, `'circle'`, `'forecourt'`, `'beach'`, `'overview'`, `'lobby-helipad'`, `'lobby-bar'`, or on a roof as the lift leaves you: `'helipad'`, `'bar'`, `'bar-counter'` |
| `cloudLayer(x, y, altitudeM)`, `cloudSpot(kind, lead)`, `cloudJump(metres, kind)` | Clouds group (clouds-console.js). The cloud layer over a map point (default the player) in metres: `baseM`/`topM` (cloudBaseAt/cloudTopAt), `cover` now, the area weights (`sea`, `mountain`, `city`, `groundM`), `amount` in cloud at `altitudeM` (default the player's), the jumper's eased `immersion`, the weather's own base and top, scud depth; with WebGL `view`: the camera's `inCloud`, the veil (`steps`, `cap`, LOW's `flat`), `wisps`, `lensWet`, subject distance, tower occluders. `cloudSpot` finds a spot within ~3 km that is thick cloud (`'cloud'`), clear (`'gap'`) or clear with cloud just north, where the camera looks (`'edge'`), `lead` seconds ahead of the drift. `cloudJump` bails out `metres` above the ground (default 700) over such a spot on land, aimed where the cloud will be when the jumper reaches it (returns the spot, the parachute state and the layer there) |
| `barriers()`, `solidAt(points, r, foot)` | Barrier audit: every sea railing run, street-end guardrail, gate pier and railing as data (what the renderer draws and `solid()` blocks); `solid()` at many `[x, y]` points at once (`foot` adds the player's foot obstacles: furniture, trunks, fixtures) |
