# Console: graphics

`addConsoleMethods('graphics', …)` in `src/game-console-graphics.js`. Graphics and render probes: quality, render scale, frame stats, post views, shadow and draw-call probes, scale audit, model lineups.

| Method | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `tyreSmoke()` | Tyre smoke, dust and road spray (tyresmoke3d.js): puffs alive, the graphics tier's cap, the pool's capacity, puffs emitted since boot and the peak alive (`null` without the 3D renderer) |
| `effectParticles()` | The effect particle pool (fx3d-particles.js: smoke, dust, fire, sparks, flashes, glass, drops in one sorted, lit, instanced draw): `live` slots, `waiting` (on a delay), `drawn` last frame and of them `game` (the game's `particles`), `capacity` and `drawCapacity`, `peak`, `emitted` since boot, `dropped` for want of room, the tier's `tierShare` of the big recipes, `drawCalls` (0 or 1: the pool's own pass after the scene), `atlas` (`baked`, CPU `ms` of the sliced bake) and `blastAge` (seconds since the last blast, null before one); `null` without the 3D renderer |
| `chimneySmoke()` | The mountain chimneys' wood smoke (chimney-smoke3d.js): `chimneys` (village houses and the 4x4 club, `club` true when its chimney is listed), `lit` (fires burning now: a fixed share by day, more at night, the club's always), `emitting` (the burning chimneys nearest the view that smoke, at most `cap` by tier), `rate` (puffs a second per plume, from the wind so the puffs stay a fixed spacing apart), `puffsPerPlume`, `liveEstimate`, `emitted` since boot, `life`, `sizeMetres` (at the pot and at the top), `alpha` (day, night; thinned by size: `thin`), `wind` (m/s, angle), `pool` (live slots, dropped); `null` without the 3D renderer |
| `scaleReport(radius)` | World-scale audit in metres: every vehicle type's spec (`l`, `w`), the built models within `radius` of the player measured from their meshes (length, width, height), the player's figure, the crowd's statures (rig at look height 1, the player, shortest, average and tallest adult pedestrian) and the building heights (lowest, median, 90th percentile, tallest) |
| `policeLineup(x, y, heading, lights, spacing)` | Park every police model and livery (pursuit sedan, utility and Crown Vic in black and white / modern / sheriff / unmarked, the agents' Tahoe, the SWAT BearCat) in a column, lights on (`true` parked at a scene, `'pursuit'`, `false`); returns ids and looks (police3d.js) |
| `carLineup(types, x, y, heading, spacing, color, lamps)` | Park one of each civilian car and motorbike type in `types` (default every one: the redesigned cars, the flagships and the bikes) in a column, each in its own or the given colour; `lamps` true lights them as if driven (riders shown on the bikes), `'brake'` holds the brake lights (cars3d.js, motorbikes3d.js) |
| `carModels()` | Every civilian car and motorbike model built (and Vinny's truck, vinnytruck3d.js): type, draw calls, shadow casters, triangles and the heaviest parts; a car's `cabin` (cars3d-interior.js): the driver's `hip` [x, y, z] in metres, the seat backs' `recline`, `rear` bench, `two`-seater, `headroom` round the tallest seated head as drawn (cars3d-headroom.js) and `behind` (headrest to rear glass, metres), `cabinTriangles` in the trim, people `seated` on its last frame in reach; `badge` {text, sub, style, letters}: the model name on the tail (cars3d-badges.js) |
| `assetCars()`, `assetCarsOn(on)` | The downloaded vehicle models in the build (vehicle-assets3d.js ASSET CARS): title, triangles, size, belt and roof per type, the kits built; `assetCarsOn(false)` makes every vehicle model built from then on procedural (park a second row to compare) |
| `cabinHeadroom()` | The seated heads against every closed car cabin built (cars3d-headroom.js CABIN HEADROOM), once per kit: `type`, `body` (police), `hip` [x, y] metres, `recline`, `fitMs` (what fitting the seat cost when the kit was built), `clear` (metres of room round the tallest man's, woman's and the player's heads inside the roof and glass; negative: through) and `roof` (the roof and panes alone), `key` / `seat` (the DRIVEBY_SEATS line it records) with the game's `gameSeat` and their `seatGap`, `through` (who pokes out where); top level `through` (cars with a head out) and `driveByArm` (the last drive-by frame: `gap` metres the gun was brought in, `window`, `relDeg`, `type`). Null without the 3D renderer |
| `carBadges()` | What every vehicle model built carries on its tail (cars3d-badges.js REAR BADGES): `id`, `type`, `body` (police), `badge` ({text, sub, style, letters} for glyph badges, `panel` for the trucks' and bus's painted tail panels, `stencil` for the army's). Null without the 3D renderer |
| `vehicleMerges()` | The PRISTINE MERGE (vehicle-merge3d.js): models merged and split so far, kits planned, draw calls saved now (`savedDraws`), `failed`, the slowest merge and kit plan in ms, `live` merged models, and why the other civilian and police models are not merged (`unmerged`: player car, wrecked, damaged, hurt, stained, mud, carjack door, not yet) |
| `vehicleMergeAudit()` | Every merged mesh against the hidden parts it replaces, vertex by vertex in world space: `maxError` (units) and `maxNormalError` must stay ~0, `worst` names the model and part (rendered pages) |
| `helicopterLineup(x, y, heading, rotors, spacing, looks)`, `helicopterModels()` | Park one helicopter of each look (police, news, executive, the civil schemes `civil:classic`, `civil:yellow`, `civil:silver`, `civil:noir`, military, or the `looks` given) in a row, rotors spun up and police lights running with `rotors` true (helicopter3d.js); every helicopter model built: look, scheme, rotor spool, draw calls, shadow casters, triangles, crew shown, livery paint time (ms) |
| `inspectView(yaw, pitch, lift)` | Inspection only: turn the street camera to a bearing (0 = from the south, as in play; 90 = from the east) and a pitch in degrees above the horizon, aimed `lift` units up; no arguments restores it. With `closeUp` it shows characters from the side |
| `vegetation()` | The trees (vegetation3d.js): `species` (street, park, county, Monarch and beach trees and palms planted, by species), `forest` (the Ridgeline's trees by species), `regions` (every tree, palm, shrub and grass clump by region — Palm Keys, Northbank, Monarch Isle, Sunset Pier, Ridgeline range / lowlands, the county coasts — and species), `landscape` (the scenery-only plantings: Keys dunes and parks, mountain road verges; meshes), `view` (tree meshes in view this frame: draw calls, shadow casters, instances, triangles, instances per mesh) and `trianglesPerTree` (each species' near `|0` and mid `|1` geometry) |
| `foliageCutaway()` | The see-through hole in tree crowns, palms and shrubs round the player (foliage-cutaway.js): `on`, `setting` (Settings · Character see-through, shared with the building cutaway), `kind` (`foot`, `vehicle`, `none`), `at`, `heightM`, `floorM` (nothing lower over the subject's base is cut), `boxM` (half size of the fully open box on the street camera's screen) and `fadeM`; `sight` (what the shader drops, 0..1, every 2 m along the street camera's line of sight from the subject's middle up to 24 m high: all 1) and `stays` (behind, beside, a grass tuft at its feet: all 0). `renderer` (null without WebGL): `strength` (eased), the tree material's `uniforms`, `crowns` crossing the camera's line of sight to the subject's middle and top within 60 m, `uncovered` (every crossing fully dropped) and the nearest `crossings` |
| `treeLineup(x, y, spacing, lod, perRow)` | Inspection only: one of every tree species in rows east of (x, y) (default the player), `lod` 0 near or 1 mid, plain instances; returns the species in order |
| `simProfile(seconds, keys, top)` | Logic cost with no drawing (`src/game-console-perf.js`): steps `update()` at 1/60 s for `seconds` (6) holding `keys`, and reports frames, avg / p50 / p95 / p99 / max ms, the `top` (14) timed sections as `[avg ms, worst ms]` (`a:b` names nest in their parent; `(untimed)` is update()'s own code), the five slowest frames with their biggest section, `slow` (frames a section alone took over 3 ms) and the JS heap growth. Read-only like `simulate()`, which it advances; use it with `node tools/dev.mjs call simProfile 8 '["KeyW"]' --cpu --profile 40` for CPU time and a function profile |
| `hitchRun(seconds, keys, top, keep, draw)` | Hiccup hunt with stepped frames (`draw` false: no drawing, a scene staged quickly on a rendered page) (`src/frame-trace.js`): runs `seconds` (6) of whole frames at 1/60 s (input, update, HUD and, on a rendered page, the draw) holding `keys`, in any mode, and reports `p50Ms` / `p95Ms` / `p99Ms` / `maxMs`, `long` (frames over twice the median and 4 ms over it), `over16` / `over33`, `causes` (the top-level section that ran furthest over its average in each long frame), `tags` (what happened in them: `gc`, `gc-between`, `program`, `texture-upload`, `geometry-upload`, `models`, `spawn`, `dom`, `storage`, `audio-nodes`, `canvas`, `sync-read`), `excessMs`, `sections` `[avg, worst]`, `totals` / `perSecond` (allocKB, gcMB, DOM mutations, texture and buffer MB uploaded, links, models, audio nodes, canvas draws and text, storage writes), `domTargets` (`id .class [attribute]`) and the `top` (10) `worst` frames with their parts and counters; `keep` appends to the last run (one report for a stage of several steps). Counters come from wrappers on the browser's prototypes while the trace runs (`wrappersLeft` is 0 after). `node tools/hitches.mjs` runs the fixed tour with it |
| `frameTrace(command, keys)` | The same record for live frames: `'start'` (holding `keys`), `'report'`, `'stop'`; adds `gaps` (requestAnimationFrame intervals: the browser's style, layout and paint land there) |
| `uploadChurn(frames, keys)` | Rendered pages: steps `frames` (30) whole frames and lists the buffer attributes and textures whose data changed (three.js uploads them again): `[owner attribute, KB (whole buffer per change, an upper bound where an update range is sent), uploads, objects]`, biggest first, and `kbPerFrame` |
| `allocBench(name, rounds)` | Garbage per call of one hot simulation function on the world as it is (changes it: a dev page): `trafficControl` (every city AI car), `controlVehicle`, `settleVehicle` (every vehicle), `vehicleBroadphase`, `knockSceneProps`, `physicsStep` (once a round): `bytesPerCall` from the JS heap's growth (exact with tools/dev.mjs's `--enable-precise-memory-info`), `usPerCall`, `roundsDropped` (a collection interrupted them) |
| `shapeReport(kind)` | Object layouts of the `vehicles` (or `people`): distinct key orders (`layouts`), the biggest (`[objects, keys]`), where the next ones part from it (`parts`), the keys added after creation (`lastKeys`), the keys some objects add (`extraKeys`) or lack (`missingKeys`). Vehicles must stay at 2-3 layouts (every field is declared in `makeCar`) |
| `soakReport()` | Long-session health (`src/game-console-soak.js`, read by `tools/soak.mjs`): `lists` (the size of every list, log, queue and cache the game appends to, by name; `null` where a binding is gone), `counts` (awake / resting vehicles, wrecks, abandoned cars, `wreckRetired` / `wreckRetiredByCap`, AI cars, car-stain entries, people reacting, fleeing, pending), `bad` (any NaN or Infinity in a position: `count`, `first`), `dom` (nodes under each top-level container) and the JS heap |
| `stateHash()`, `seedRandom(seed)` | A 16-digit fingerprint of the positions, velocities and health of the player, vehicles, people, armed lists and effects, plus the wanted level, cash and clock; and a seeded `Math.random`: the same seeded play (the same calls and steps) gives the same hash on every run and build, so a performance change that must not change behaviour is proved by equal hashes (`tools/soak.mjs` records one per sample) |
| `hypotAudit(count)`, `cellMaskAudit()`, `solidBench(count, span, r)` | Checks and a benchmark for the hot-path helpers: `hypot2` (game-state.js) against `Math.hypot` bit for bit and its speed per million calls; the promenade rail grid's cell mask against the grid over the whole map (`mismatches` 0); `count` `solid()` calls at fixed pseudo-random points (time and, with `--alloc`, garbage per call) |
| `rainView()` | The rain and the wet street as drawn (weather3d-chase.js): `street` (the chase view's numbers in use), the street box (`drops`, `fallMetres`, `streakMetres`, `opacity`, `lensClearMetres`), the `near` box at street level, `splashes` (count, reach, size, `upright` crowns), the `haze` (`clearMetres`, `farMetres`, `skyLeft`: the haze colour's sum after the rain's darkening), the wet `reflections` (on, mirror, sky share, reach, Fresnel, jitter share, first step) and `sheenRadial`; null without WebGL |
| `wetGlints()` | The street lamps mirrored in the wet road this frame (wet-glints3d.js WET LAMP GLINTS): `slots` (40) and the tier's `tierSlots` (LOW 0, MEDIUM 20), `sources` and `extras` (`lamps`, `signs`, `moving`: bridge leaf lamps) registered with addWetGlint, `signs` in the list now and `signSlots`, `count` in the uniforms, `candidates` (lamps within `reach` of the view or still fading out), `chaseChecks` (lamps tested this frame for a building between the chase camera and the head) and `hidden`, `gain`, `lampPower`, `wet`, `intensity`, the `nearest` six (`x`, `y`, head `height`, faded `strength`) and `lightMapStreaks` (false: the light map is no longer smeared into the road); `null` without WebGL |
| `stats()` | Per-frame CPU timings (`parts`, the renderer's split as `r:` parts), draw calls (`viewCalls` camera, `shadowCalls` shadow map, redrawn every frame while shadows are on), triangles, linked shader `programs`, the dynamic `renderScale` |
| `bootTimings()` | The boot timeline (read-only): `page` (navigation timings) and `marks` as `[name, ms since navigation start, ms since the step before]` for every `bootMark()`: `loader`, `game-start`, `world`, `colliders`, `populated`, `title-menu`, `visuals-queued`, `loop-ready`, `console-ready`, `first-frame`, `images`, `scene-built`, `batched`, `far-scenery`, `cells-lamps`, `renderer`, `prewarm-done`, and after ENTER `begin`, `audio-init`, `first-play-frame` (docs/areas/boot-and-memory.md) |
| `adaptiveSim(plan)` | Feeds AUTO's dynamic-resolution controller a synthetic run (tests; needs the 3D renderer and the AUTO setting): `plan` is `[[seconds, frameMs, cpuMs], ...]` on a clock of its own; returns `scale`, `tier`, `tierDrops`, `ceiling` and every change as `[seconds, scale]` (each is a reallocation of the post targets). It moves the real adaptive state: `graphics('auto')` resets it |
| `renderHiccups(reset)` | What each drawn frame created for the first time (render3d-hiccups.js): `programsLinked`, `texturesCreated`, `geometriesCreated` since the log began (`reset` true starts a fresh one), `firstUseFrames`, `worstCpuMs` (a frame's renderer CPU without the GPU submit) and `worstSubmitMs`, the 8 slowest frames and the last 40 first-use `events` (frame, game time, `cpu`, `submit`, `laps` = the `r:` split, `programs` with `names` as `Type:uniforms @owner ~variant[cache-key fields that differ from an older program of the same shader]`, `textures`, `geometries`, the `lights` the programs were keyed by); `now` and `memory` (programs, textures, geometries held by the GPU), `lights` (the light counts three.js keys programs by), `gpu` (every geometry and texture the scene could draw against those uploaded: a first draw uploads the rest), `cells` (geometry MB per batch cell, static cell and the far copy), `prewarm` (the title-screen shader prewarm: `parallel` driver compile, objects, passes, stand-in models and meshes still queued, programs compiled and linked, CPU `ms`, `done`, `error`), `litStage` (the staged tier change: `staging` available, `pending`, `switches` made, programs `compiled`, CPU `ms`, `error`) and `cells.preUpload` (the pre-upload ahead of the view: meshes and MB sent, slices, the slowest slice in ms, cells listed) |
| `searchlight(options)` | The police helicopter's searchlight (searchlight3d.js): active, state, aim, lag behind the target, pool radius, altitude, irradiance and exposure, `onCover` / `roof` / `roofStandIn` / `landing` / `floor` (the light landing on a roof over the target), the shaft's uniforms and whether it is shown (HIGH/ULTRA), the spot shadow size, the aim and the player on screen and `pixelsPerUnit` (for contrast measurements); `{ shaft: false }` / `{ pool: false }` switch parts off for A/B shots (`true` restores) |
| `headlights()` | Vehicle lights this frame (lighting3d-vehicle-lights.js): `slots` (the tier's CAR LAMPS), `analytic` (cars lighting the world as real lights, the player's first) with each car's lamp point (`x`, `y` up, `z`), height, strength, body frame (`pitch`, `roll` in degrees, `aim` forward vector in map x, y, up), `horizon` (reads the terrain horizon) and `lightBar` (the club truck's flood slot), `horizonsComputed` (terrain horizons recomputed this frame), `terrainBeams`, `driveHeads` (beams in the drive light map), `tails`, `reversing`, police `strobes`, beam `haze` sheets and `hazeLevel`, `shadowWedges` (cars, people, trees, posts and furniture shadowing the beams) and `shadowSlots` (slots whose beams they shadow, BEAM SHADOWS), `lampsOn`, `headBeamPeak` (the drive map beam's raw peak) |
| `headlightBudget(lamps, points)` | Low beams on a level road through the VEHICLE LIGHT BUDGET (headlight-beam.js; works without the renderer): `lamps` `[[x, y, heading], ...]` lamp points, `points` `[[x, y], ...]`; per point `total` (what the road takes from them all), `brightest` (one beam alone), `sum` (the beams added one on another: the old junction white-out), `beams`; `cap`, `reachM` |
| `headlightAim(metres)` | The player's lamps on the ground they stand on (terrain-headlights.js; works without the renderer): `pitchDeg`, `rollDeg`, `aim` and `up` (body frame, map x, y, up), `lampHeightM`, `lampOverGroundM`, `horizon` (terrain in reach), `horizonMs` (the time one horizon took), and for points on the ground straight ahead (`metres`, default 5-50) the ground height (m), the beam coordinates in the body frame (`t` to the kerb side, `v` above the lamps' axis: the low beam lights about -0.01 to -0.2), `vLevel` (the old level frame) and `lit` (the terrain horizon: 0 behind a hill) |
| `lookSwitches(options)` | A/B switches for the look, all `true` in play: `vehicleMerge` (false: every car drawn from its own parts, the PRISTINE MERGE off), `pixelLock` (street camera snapped to pixels), `fxaa` (FXAA after MSAA on HIGH/ULTRA), `vibrance`, `carLamps` (false: every car through the drive map), `groundSlopeCap` (the ground bump's tilt cap), `terrainBeams` (false: beams level with the car's heading, no terrain horizon, light bar or mountain haze), `lightBar` (the club truck's flood on the range), `foliageCoverage` (alpha to coverage for the tree cut-outs on the MSAA tiers; false: the hard alpha test), `vehicleLights` (false: no vehicle lights the world: beams, drive map, washes), `stagedSwitch` (false: a tier or shadow change flips the lit flags at once instead of staging them: the A/B for the relink hitch), `roofSkin` (false: the roofs' old whole-roof paintings instead of the roof skin, roofskin3d.js), `wallSkin` (false: the facades without the WALL SKIN's real brick, concrete, steel and plaster, cityscape3d-wallskin.js), `groundWear` (false: the asphalt without the resurfacing stretches, thermal cracks, lane joints, trenches and junction wear), `chaseBudget` (false: the chase view's old level of detail: every static group's part, sign, small batch, vehicle part and effect sprite drawn and casting, no haze sizes or per-batch shadow tests; rendering-chase-budget.md), `wetGlints` (false: no street lamps mirrored in the wet road, wet-glints3d.js), `wetReflections` (false: the wet reflections pass off, as on MEDIUM); returns the switches |
| `wallSkin()` | The WALL SKIN (cityscape3d-wallskin.js): whether assets/wall-skin.webp loaded (`ready`), the switch, layer size, each layer's name, ambientCG source, tile in metres, linear mean colour and how many facade materials use it, and whether the normals are drawn (MEDIUM and up); null without WebGL |
| `postView(mode)` | Show the ambient-occlusion (`'ao'`), bloom (`'bloom'`), depth (`'depth'`, banded) or wet-reflection (`'reflect'`: the reflection around mid grey, the ground's wet reflectivity in red; HIGH / ULTRA in the wet) buffer instead of the image; no argument restores it |
| `groundDetail()` | The ground materials (ground-data3d.js, grass3d.js): build time (ms), detail layer, carriageway field and marks bytes, the marks (records, filed copies in the 32-unit grid, any dropped from a full cell), the tier's detail level (0 LOW .. 3 ULTRA), the grass tufts (shown, instances, zoom fade) and `roofs` (roofskin3d.js: the roof finishes in use, buildings tinted, roof caps per finish) |

| `drawProfile(top)` | Draw calls in view by object name and by 512-unit map cell, `byLayer` (calls on the small-prop and mid-prop detail layers against the rest) (for finding unbatched scenery; unnamed parts of anonymous groups are listed with geometry, material and colour), the camera pass's triangles in total and by the same names (`triangles`, `byTriangles`: an instanced pool counts every instance), and linked shader programs by kind |

| `renderScale(scale)` | Draw the scene at `scale` (0.5..1) of the canvas, as AUTO's dynamic resolution does; returns the scale applied |
