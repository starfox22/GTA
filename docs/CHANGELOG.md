### Carjack struggle, voices that match faces, backpedalling

- Taking an occupied car is now a short struggle (about 1-2 s): you walk to the driver's
  door (round the car from the kerb), yank it open, haul the driver out in a brief tug of
  war (they cling to the wheel, flail; a tough one lands a punch; some give up hands up),
  swing them clear and take the seat. E again cuts it short; walking off calls it off.
- Victims get up and shout something that fits them ("Police! Someone took my car!",
  "My purse is in there!", "Young man, that is my car!"), then phone it in once clear of
  you (the witness system); some chase you a few steps first. Passengers bail out of the
  far door and run.
- Screams now match the person: men scream in a man's voice, women in a woman's, children
  higher, the old lower; crowds (the stadium, the beach, the coaster) scream as the real
  people in them. Women officers no longer speak with the recorded male police voice.
- Backpedalling while aiming: the legs step backwards (or side-step) instead of running
  the wrong way, with a shorter stride; you face the aim while fighting and turn to run
  otherwise. Backpedalling is at 60% of the pace, side-stepping 80%. Officers and gangs
  backing off while firing step the same way.
- Internals: voices.js (personFemale, one sex rule shared with the rig), footwork.js,
  carjack-struggle.js, carjack3d.js. Console: `voiceReport`, `screamTest`, `footwork`,
  `carjackTest`, `carjack`. Tests: voices, footwork, carjack.

### Manual parachute and lethal falls

- The parachute never opens by itself: after jumping, press J (the bail key) again. The press
  that jumped does not count; it has to be let go first. Unopened, you hit the ground at
  freefall speed: a thud, the camera jolts, the body lies face down in a spreading pool.
- A freefall cue under the player: PRESS J TO OPEN PARACHUTE, the height above the ground and
  a bar against the room the canopy needs (about 55 m from full speed); it pulses, amber when
  it is time, red below the safe height. The canopy takes 2.4 s to open; opened too low it
  lands hard or kills. Roofs count as ground; water is fatal from 30 m/s. Touch: the jump
  button reads OPEN CHUTE in freefall. Space no longer opens the canopy.
- Falls on foot: walking or tumbling off a drop steeper than 54° is a real fall. Under 6 m a
  stumble, 6-17 m an injury, beyond that death; faces over 45° take you down with them.
- Cars off cliffs fly (pitching over the edge, rolling if taken at an angle), land by their
  speed into the ground: dents, a hard landing, a roll onto the roof or side, or a burning
  wreck that kills whoever is inside. Riders are thrown. Roads and trails stay safe.
- Console group `falls`: `bailOut`, `openParachute`, `parachuteState`, `fallState`,
  `cliffSpot`, `fallTest` (docs/console/world.md). Tests: parachute.mjs, falls.mjs.

### Witnesses and 911 calls

- The police only come when they know: a crime nobody saw or heard brings no stars. A unit that sees it (or hears the shots) responds at once, as before.
- Civilians who saw it (in their field of view) or heard the shots duck and run, then get clear and phone 911 with a speech bubble ("911? There's been a shooting on Ocean Drive!"). The first star comes only when the call ends ("A WITNESS CALLED 911"); kill, threaten or reach the caller and the call is lost.
- Dispatch sends units to the reported spot after a response time that grows with how remote it is (seconds downtown, longer in the county and on Monarch Isle); they spawn off screen, search, and take up the chase only on sight. Escalation waits until they arrive.
- A carjacked driver (and a Trail Club member watching their truck go) phones it in once back on their feet; a body found later still gets reported; a body found long after with the player gone is only investigated.
- Street life: context chatter by hour, weather and district, remarks about a gun on show, blood, a showy or stolen car; more varied panic, filming, near-miss and onlooker lines; onlookers comfort each other; no person repeats a line twice running and idle remarks are spaced out.
- Internals: witnesses.js (unreported crimes, police eyes and ears, reports, response time, `witnessReport()` API), crowd-witnesses.js (witness memory, the call director, the call), crowd-chatter.js (lines and pickers); `crime(amount, how)` takes `'seen'` for scripted crimes.
- Console: `witnesses()`, `witnessStage(count, police, radius, lookAway)`, `carjackTarget(type, mood)`. Test: tools/tests/police-witness.mjs.

### Scenic mountain roads and regional vegetation

- Ridgeline's roads (Ridgeline Highway, Eagle Pass, Regency Road and the connectors) are real mountain roads: smooth curves instead of squared corners, climbing and falling with the hills at up to 8% with long vertical curves, banked through the bends, on cuttings and embankments.
- A crisp asphalt ribbon with white edge lines, a yellow centre line (double through bends and junctions), gravel shoulders, patches and a wet sheen; guard rails where the ground drops away, reflector posts, three viewpoint lay-bys with a bench, a coin telescope and a SCENIC VIEW sign.
- A car on a mountain road has tarmac grip (it used to be treated as rough dirt near the peaks); the terrain panel reads MOUNTAIN ROAD · grade · height.
- Vegetation by region: dune grass and sea grape on the Palm Keys beach, hibiscus and bougainvillea in the Keys' parks, royal palms along Monarch Isle's boulevards, palms, stone pines and sea grape on the county's resort coast, oak woodland and groves in the lowlands, forest stands of one kind in the range, pines along the sea, meadow grass on the mountain roads' verges.
- Italian cypress is one smooth column; the Bradford pear is a fuller, brighter tree; the far forest keeps each tree's form (pines no longer spruce cones); county road verges are a dusty olive, not pale gravel.
- No tree stands on a carriageway, in a building, under a rail deck, on a runway or in a doorway.
- Internals: terrain-roads.js / terrain-grading.js / terrain-roadside.js (filleted centrelines kept out of towns, spline profiles, carve with embankment faces under the fall grade, furniture, colliders), county3d-roads.js (ribbon and furniture), vegetation3d-landscape.js; `onCountyRoad` is bucketed.

### Night headlights, steadier textures and a richer daylight look

- Headlights at night: the player's car and the nearest cars (1 on LOW, 4 MEDIUM, 8 HIGH, 12 ULTRA) now light the world with a real low-beam pattern (hot spot, sharp cut-off rising on the kerb side, long throw along the pavement); kerbs, walls, parked cars and pedestrians in front are lit on the faces that face the car, wet tarmac and paint catch the glare. People and cars standing in the player's beams cast shadows along them. Other traffic lays the same beam shape on the road.
- Lamp glows brighten into a bloom glow (and a faint flare streak on HIGH/ULTRA) when they face the camera; brake lights flare brighter and wash the road redder; reversing cars show white lamps and a white pool behind them; rain streaks glitter in the beams and a soft haze hangs in them in rain and mist (HIGH/ULTRA).
- The player's headlight no longer shines by day (the old spotlight was always on).
- Texture shimmer on HIGH/ULTRA: the street camera is locked to whole pixels (static scenery no longer crawls while the view scrolls), FXAA now runs after the tone curve on every tier (MSAA left bright edges, leaf cut-outs and glints stepped and flickering), the ground's bump no longer flips bright/dark fringes along markings and kerbs as the view moves, and the half-resolution ambient occlusion is upsampled depth-aware (no shade fringes round cars, people and roof edges).
- Daylight: warmer sun and a cooler, deeper sky fill, cool shade and warm highlights, vibrance instead of flat saturation, bloom only on glints by day (the pale paving no longer hangs a milky veil), ambient occlusion kept on sunlit ground.
- Internals: lighting3d-cutaway.js split into cutaway / vehicle-lights / look; CAR LAMPS uniform arrays (`cityCarLampA/B`, `cityCarLampCount`) in `CITY_LIGHT_PARS`; `queueVehicleHalo(sprite, opacity, facing, gain, tint, size)`; quality tiers gain `carLamps`.
- New console methods: `headlights()` (vehicle lights this frame), `lookSwitches(options)` (A/B switches: pixelLock, fxaa, vibrance, carLamps, groundSlopeCap).
