# M4 utility: working lenses, matte finishes and shared geometry

- The downloaded M4's head/tail lenses now use working, damageable lamp materials instead of dark window glass.
  Its original lens triangles and textured model stay intact; coated body/canvas and tyres have matte finishes.
- M4 and M35 front wheels steer with the physics ramp. Roll follows their actual radii, brake lock, reverse and rear wheelspin.
- M4 wheel pivots now match their actual tyre circles; fixed calipers/running gear stay with the body instead of spinning.
- M4 lamp lenses use actual daylight/night, driven/parked, braking and damage states. The rendered prototype's always-lit parked headlights were corrected before publication.
  All original triangles remain at rest; the rear tyres no longer orbit an axle about 35 cm off-centre.
- Military geometry kits are reused across spawns. The intact near M4 body stays unclustered; far LOD has hysteresis.
- Single-draw packed wheels distinguish rubber from painted hubs with vertex finishes; wear, burn and repair preserve the contract.
- Records-office searches and skyline lift rides release held aiming as well as firing/movement; the regression uses the actual `mouse.alt` right-button state.
- New production-function checks: `military-model-rig` and `mission-modal-input`; actual rendered evidence is in the October 13 audit.
