# Keep mission work grounded through jumps and vehicle impacts

- High Ground digging pauses immediately through a jump, fall, tumble or knockdown, keeps earlier progress, and resumes after the player lands. Loading and handing over the package require stable footing too.
- Borrowed Stripes no longer lets the player recover the uniform, change clothes, show ID or enter Records while swimming, falling or jumping. A hit during the gate conversation interrupts it instead of clearing a player lying on the ground.
- Changing inside a vehicle requires its actual velocity to settle, with the vehicle upright, grounded and above water.
- Production-function interruption regression and real jump/interact checks cover these paths alongside the existing mission lifecycle tests.
