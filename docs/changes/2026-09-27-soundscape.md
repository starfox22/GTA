# Free roam sounds like a place
- Gunfire, explosions and crashes ring off the street: a slap-back between the facades
  downtown, a long rolling echo in the hills; a fight behind a building is a muffled thud
  and its echo, far shots lose their crack. Under the underpass or in a garage the engine
  and your steps boom.
- Every place has its bed, cross-faded as you move: the city's far roar (louder up on a
  roof), gusting wind that grows in the open, on the beach, on towers and up the range
  (whistling on the heights), leaves in the parks, cicadas on warm afternoons and insects
  at night, rigging, creaking timber and ship horns in the harbours, doves, crows, a
  woodpecker in the county's woods, a hawk over the peaks, frogs by the reservoir,
  Monarch Isle's sprinklers and a far mower. A closed car dulls the street.
- Footsteps follow the ground: asphalt, pavement, tile, roofs, boardwalk and pier planks,
  the drawbridge's steel, grass, sand, trail dirt and mud, rock, scree and snow; puddles
  in the wet, a run's rustle, a landing on both feet. Officers chasing you and people
  fleeing are heard running.
- Horns by class (a car's two-tone, a truck's air horn, a bike's beep, a boat's horn, a
  bicycle bell) for you and traffic; car doors open and shut as you get in and out, a
  locked handle rattles; tyres squeal only on hard ground and scrub on sand, grass and
  dirt; sliding traffic and police cars squeal; the siren pans and Doppler-shifts. Kerbs, rocks,
  ruts and landings knock through the suspension; fast cars whoosh past.
- Bullets chip concrete, ricochet, knock on metal, crack glass and thud into dirt; enemy
  rounds whizz and crack past you. The car radio's duck under callouts glides.
- The mix: the street dips for a moment under a nearby shot or blast and swells back; a
  brick-wall ceiling after the limiter; near thunder rolls off the hills and slaps between
  the towers; beeps no longer click in.
- Console: `acoustics(x, y)`, `soundscape()`, `footsteps(x, y)`, `vehicleFoley()`
  (settings group); test tools/tests/soundscape.mjs.
