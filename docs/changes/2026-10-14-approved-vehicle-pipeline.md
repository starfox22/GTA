# Approved vehicle pipeline

Owner approval covers five free replacement models from the October 14 shortlist.
A separate packed asset set preserves the previous 23 models. The runtime retains
repeat-safe source albedo and roughness/metallic channels, respray paint and projected
sheriff livery through near/far geometry, wear, char and repair. Shared texture objects
switch between 2048px Low/Medium and 4096px High/Ultra images. SUV collision envelopes
match the three civilian source bodies through cached specs without consuming RNG.
Credits are embedded in the standalone game; conversion and visual validation are
documented in `docs/audit/approved-vehicle-integration-2026-10-14/README.md`.
