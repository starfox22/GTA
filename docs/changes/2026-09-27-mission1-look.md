# Mission 1's look: Vinny's truck and the yellow payphone
- Vinny's truck is a real-size 1990s ATLAS medium-duty flatbed: set-back hood between flared
  fenders, chrome bar grille and bumper, twin rectangular head lamps, two-piece windscreen and
  sun visor with five amber cab markers, West Coast mirrors, polished fuel tank step, exhaust
  stack, air horns, mesh headache rack, hardwood deck with stake pockets and conspicuity tape,
  underride bar, mud flaps and dual rear wheels on steel discs; MORETTI & SONS door lettering
  and fleet number 07 on the roof. Markers light with the lamps; the hazards blink while it
  waits for you; the front wheels steer. 85 draw calls down to about 17.
- The crane lowers each crate onto its own slot on the bed and turns it to the truck.
- The story payphone is a yellow 1990s pedestal payphone with a PHONE light box (lit at
  night, glowing on the pavement), a handset on an armoured cord that trembles while the call
  waits, keypad, placards, stickers and a directory; newspaper boxes either side; litter,
  leaves, weeds and a storm drain round it; three cars parked in the lot behind it.
- Internals: vinnytruck3d.js (`makeVinnyTruck`, civilian lamp/wheel contract, `VINNY_CARGO`
  in harbor-terminal.js), payphone3d.js; `carModels()` also reports the truck.
