# Manual parachute and lethal falls

- The parachute never opens by itself: after jumping, press J (the bail key) again. The press
  that jumped does not count; it has to be let go first. Unopened, you hit the ground at
  freefall speed: a thud, the camera jolts, the body lies face down in a spreading pool.
- A freefall cue under the player: PRESS J TO OPEN PARACHUTE, the height above the ground and
  a bar against the room the canopy needs (about 55 m from full speed); it pulses, amber when
  it is time, red below the safe height. The canopy takes 2.4 s to open; opened too low it
  lands hard or kills. Roofs count as ground; water is fatal from 30 m/s. Touch: the jump
  button reads OPEN CHUTE in freefall. Space no longer opens the canopy.
- Falls on foot: walking or tumbling off a drop steeper than 54° is a real fall. Under 6 m a
  stumble, 6-17 m an injury, beyond that death; faces over 45° take you down with them.
- Cars off cliffs fly (pitching over the edge, rolling if taken at an angle), land by their
  speed into the ground: dents, a hard landing, a roll onto the roof or side, or a burning
  wreck that kills whoever is inside. Riders are thrown. Roads and trails stay safe.
- Console group `falls`: `bailOut`, `openParachute`, `parachuteState`, `fallState`,
  `cliffSpot`, `fallTest` (docs/console/world.md). Tests: parachute.mjs, falls.mjs.
