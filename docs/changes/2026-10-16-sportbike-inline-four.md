# KUZUKI600R: recorded Japanese inline-four engine

- The sport motorcycle now has a real Kawasaki ZX6R tickover and Suzuki sportsbike exhaust recording, replacing its twin-cylinder idle and car-engine loops.
- It pulls through six close ratios to 14,500 rpm shifts and a 15,500 rpm ceiling, with brief shift cuts, wheel-driven engine braking, load-sensitive induction and a regular audio-rate ignition limiter.
- Dolcati, Yamasaki, the cruiser and dirt bike retain their existing sound identities. The stock sport four no longer inherits random V8-style backfires.
- Both recordings are CC0, pinned and credited. The Suzuki source is a pitch-stabilized track flyby derivative with mild baked reverb; it is not a claimed GSX-R600 dyno capture.
