# Carjacking: a real struggle at the door again; no dizzy stars
- Taking a car from its driver is a proper scene again: walk round to the driver's door, a hand on the handle, the door swung open on its hinge, a lean in for the collar, a one-to-two-second tug of war (both bodies rocking, the driver gripping the wheel and fighting back: an angry one shoves you off, a defiant one punches), then the driver hauled out by the collar and swung clear. Angry drivers hit the ground; frightened ones stagger away backwards and run. You step in and the door is pulled shut behind you. The camera eases in close while it happens.
- Traffic that is still rolling (over 24 km/h) or hemmed in by parked cars or the next lane no longer pops the driver out instantly: the driver brakes and you get a shorter version of the same scene. The walk to the door goes round whichever end of the car has room, squeezing through tight gaps.
- The circling stars over people lying on the ground are gone (3D and 2D).
- Internals: phases approach > door > reach > tug > pull > enter in carjack-struggle.js (`planCarjack`, `job.quick`); new poses carjackReachIn, carjackHauled, carjackStagger; stagger in carjack.js reactions. Only bikes, boats and aircraft still use the instant `ejectDriver`.
- Console: `carjack()` reports `phases`, `quick`, `cancelled`; new `carjackScan(radius, count, from)` and `carjackStage(hem, mood, side, kmh)`. Test: tools/tests/carjack-traffic.mjs (real action key at traffic at a red light, parked cars, the next lane, boxed in, rolling).
