- Replaced the procedural DOLCATI V4 with ROY's realistic, downloaded CC BY 4.0 Ducati Panigale V4 model, retaining source fairing, engine, brakes, rims, cockpit, artwork and windscreen.

- Close source detail is shared across instances; reduced traffic geometry and 1024px textures serve Low/Medium and distant bikes. Source paint artwork survives resprays; original vehicle packs remain unchanged.
