# Carjack struggle, voices that match faces, backpedalling

- Taking an occupied car is now a short struggle (about 1-2 s): you walk to the driver's
  door (round the car from the kerb), yank it open, haul the driver out in a brief tug of
  war (they cling to the wheel, flail; a tough one lands a punch; some give up hands up),
  swing them clear and take the seat. E again cuts it short; walking off calls it off.
- Victims get up and shout something that fits them ("Police! Someone took my car!",
  "My purse is in there!", "Young man, that is my car!"), then phone it in once clear of
  you (the witness system); some chase you a few steps first. Passengers bail out of the
  far door and run.
- Screams now match the person: men scream in a man's voice, women in a woman's, children
  higher, the old lower; crowds (the stadium, the beach, the coaster) scream as the real
  people in them. Women officers no longer speak with the recorded male police voice.
- Backpedalling while aiming: the legs step backwards (or side-step) instead of running
  the wrong way, with a shorter stride; you face the aim while fighting and turn to run
  otherwise. Backpedalling is at 60% of the pace, side-stepping 80%. Officers and gangs
  backing off while firing step the same way.
- Internals: voices.js (personFemale, one sex rule shared with the rig), footwork.js,
  carjack-struggle.js, carjack3d.js. Console: `voiceReport`, `screamTest`, `footwork`,
  `carjackTest`, `carjack`. Tests: voices, footwork, carjack.
