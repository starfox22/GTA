# Keep action prompts clear of dialogue and terrain status
- Action prompts dock early when their fresh position would cover dialogue, and stay docked for that action. They move directly into place instead of sliding across the words.
- Desktop docking clears the visible terrain-status chip. Touch docking keeps its existing placement.
- The frozen production DOM review covers fresh and timed-dock prompts alongside subtitles, lower HUD and terrain status at four desktop sizes.
