# Rings off the world

- No more gold ring or light pool on the ground at mission objectives: the small floating arrow is the only pointer (the map, minimap and distance pill are unchanged). Mission 1's glowing loading pad at the harbour bay is gone.
- Blue Hour: no ring round the reserved glass; once the glass is spiked nothing points at Vescari while the poison works (the arrow returns if the cover is blown or he falls).
- The ring under your character is now a setting (Settings, Graphics, Ring under your character), off by default, also for older saves.
- 2D fallback: objective, garage and dock markers lose their ground ellipse; the player circle follows the setting.
- Internals: `playerRing` saved setting (`settings({ playerRing })`), `objectiveArrowShown()` / `roofWatchQuiet()`, console `markers()`, test tools/tests/markers-rings.mjs. Removed `objectiveRing`, `targetLight`, `harborBayRing`.
