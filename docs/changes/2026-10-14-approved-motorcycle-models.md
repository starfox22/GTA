# Approved motorcycle replacements

- Use the approved M1 enduro for KR 500 and M2 sport motorcycle for VORTEX 900, with
  source materials/UVs and separate glass. Cruiser and bicycle models stay distinct.
- Preserve measured fork, rolling axles, tyre-first damage, front/rear mud contacts,
  paired rider grips, signed rolling, braking, wheelspin and repair behaviour.
- Cache model geometry by source and size. Reduced parts swap on existing meshes by
  screen scale and graphics preset with hysteresis; the player's bike remains detailed.
- Ordinary overhead NPCs use reduced meshes on all four presets, keeping the detailed
  sport model out of the city-wide traffic budget.
- Marked/dented bikes keep the near source triangles so damage decals remain attached.
- Preserve the original assets and procedural fallback when the approved pack is absent.
- Measure front driver saddle contacts from actual upper faces and fit natural enduro
  and sport-bike poses without extending character arms or moving the grip contacts.
- Keep the enduro headlamp on its fork and sport fairing lamps fixed; broken/absent
  motorcycle lenses mask their halos, and repairs restore them.
