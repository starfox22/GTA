# Chase camera feel: lock-on, look behind, motion blur, close quarters
- Lock-on (Settings · Gameplay · Aim assist, on by default): aiming with a gunman near the reticle turns the
  camera onto them and holds it; a move of the mouse or the stick breaks it (console `gunmanAt(x, y)`).
- C held in a vehicle looks out of the back (a cut round and back; Settings · Controls · Look behind).
- Camera motion blur on HIGH and ULTRA (Settings · Graphics · Motion blur; off with Motion comfort): the street
  smears with the camera's own motion while the player and their car stay sharp.
- Backed against a wall, the camera cranes up over the player's head instead of filling the screen with it.
- The car radio only flashes its station chip on getting in or a new station (as on touch screens) instead of
  opening over the road ahead; hover or a click still opens it.
- Tests: chase-lock, chase-motion-blur, chase-crane, chase-radio-chip.
