# Recover the mountain package from a stranded vehicle

- High Ground now offers RETRIEVE THE PACKAGE at the rear of its stopped carrier, so a damaged or stranded truck does not force the player to abandon the job.
- Retrieval puts the case in the backpack for travel on foot or in another vehicle, restores borrowed vehicle ownership, and keeps the recovered package safe if the old ride burns later.
- The player must be grounded, close to the rear and at the cargo's elevation; the carrier must still be alive and on the ground. Destroying it before recovery still fails the mission.
- Production recovery tests cover ownership and failure boundaries; browser checks use real interaction presses to stow, retrieve and continue in a different ride.
