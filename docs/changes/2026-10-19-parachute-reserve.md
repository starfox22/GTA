# Main cutaway and reserve parachute

- The existing remappable **Bail out / parachute / cutaway** action remains J by default. Each release and fresh press jumps, deploys the main, cuts away a fully open main, then deploys the single reserve. Holding the action through a deployment cannot cut the canopy or trigger an automatic reserve.
- Cutaway preserves the jumper's position, heading and all velocity components. Gravity resumes against body drag; there is no position reset, instant terminal velocity or mid-air braking bonus.
- The reserve uses its own quicker spring-pilot extraction followed by lines, snivel, slider run and opening shock. The same drag/load model drives the descent and the on-screen opening-height forecast. Pulling too low can still be fatal. The reserve is the last canopy and cannot be cut or reset in flight.
- The main remains visible drifting away for up to ten game seconds, using one frozen, disposable mesh copy. A world reset or a new rig clears it. The jumper eases from the hanging harness into a freefall arch, and the reserve uses white/teal panels. Existing landing collapse, ship/roof/water landing and body injury paths remain shared.
- The chase camera rises with the paying-out lines to frame the whole jumper and wing together. Its 12 m open-canopy boom widens for portrait screens; a quicker, aspect-aware extension keeps pace with the reserve's shorter inflation. Existing wall/ground clearance remains authoritative, and an open canopy uses the normal wall-release speed. Cutaway smoothly returns to the existing freefall framing; foot and aircraft shapes are unchanged.
- The controls settings, quick-key strip, touch button, freefall cue and opening messages explain CUT AWAY and OPEN RESERVE using the bound action. A later aircraft bailout starts with a fresh main and one reserve; landing removes the airborne rig.
- `tools/tests/parachute-reserve.mjs` runs the production controls, chute update/forecast and fall-impact scale in an explicit empty level-site host. Its 56 assertions cover 30/60/120 Hz, held/released input, exact momentum conservation at release, staged reserve opening, forecast accuracy, reserve exhaustion, landing cleanup, fresh rig supply, low fatal pulls and remapped input. Browser/native evidence is recorded separately in the flight review.
- `tools/tests/parachute-camera.mjs` projects the actual Three.js canopy, line, pilot and rigging vertices through the production chase camera. It checks complete main/reserve framing while trimmed and banked at 1000×700, 960×600, 390×844 and 360×800; actual growing deployments at 30/60 Hz; smooth line-stretch/cutaway transitions; wall clearance and recovery; and unchanged freefall, foot and aircraft framing.
- Fixed a native-review defect where skipping the reserve's opening frames left its visible skin in the main's colors: palette changes now copy and mark the live skin color attribute for GPU upload immediately. `parachute-palette.mjs` checks all 2142 skin/250 rib vertices through repeated full-open main/reserve switches, upload versions, unchanged geometry/materials and the released main's independent colors (37 assertions).
