# Free-roam feel: drifts, camera, tyre smoke, recoil
- Drifts: floored through a bend, a tail-happy car (the hot rod, or any car with TCS and ESC
  off) slides into a drift and holds it instead of spinning; counter-steer or a lift catches it.
- Handbrake turns bite: a half-second flick at 55 km/h swings a sedan ~40 degrees (was ~18),
  held it makes a 180. Road tyres slide on the beach's sand and the parks' lawns.
- Camera: the view leads where the car is going, not where its nose points (spins no longer
  whip it round), and holds back with police on your tail; on foot it leads the run and, in a
  fight, leans toward the aim; follow and shake are smooth at any frame rate.
- Kicks: gunfire kicks the view against the aim, crashes lurch it into what you hit, blasts
  shove it away, hits and hard landings jolt it.
- Tyre smoke from slides, burnouts, locked wheels and the handbrake; dust on sand and grass;
  spray off the wheels on soaked roads. Spent cases bounce and lie a moment.
- On foot, the police's "fire only when on screen" box follows the view as it leans.
- Console `driftTest`, `cameraFeel`, `tyreSmoke`; tests drift-handling, camera-feel.
