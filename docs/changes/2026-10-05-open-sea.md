# Open sea: return to the city, or a missile
- Flying over land (the county's east coast lies almost on the old edge line) never shows a warning any more, and
  the card never mentions a world edge. Only after 10 s flying or sailing away from all land (beyond 150 m out) does
  RETURN TO THE CITY appear with a 10 s countdown, the distance to the nearest land and an arrow to it.
- Heading back for land winds the seconds back up (a calm blue HEADING BACK card) and clears it; hovering or
  cruising along a coast never counts. Far past the map (875 m beyond the old line) the countdown starts at once.
- At zero a missile is fired from the coast side (MISSILE INBOUND, a lock-on beep): it homes in and kills the player
  in whatever they are in: plane, helicopter, boat, swimming or under a canopy. God mode is only warned.
- The flat far sea now follows the view, so open water reaches the horizon out there.
- Internals: world-edge.js (`worldEdge.out`, `worldEdgeLandDistance`, `worldEdge.missile`, drawn as a tracer in
  render3d-frame.js and game-draw2d.js); console `worldEdge()` gains `out`, `landMetres`, `awaySpeed`, `back`,
  `missile`. Tests: tools/tests/world-edge.mjs (rewritten), world-edge-land.mjs.
