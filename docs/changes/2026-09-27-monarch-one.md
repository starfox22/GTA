# Monarch One on its own point, and a garage on every island

- Monarch Isle has one skyscraper: MONARCH ONE, now a 74-storey supertall (~300 m to the
  mast) on the island's north-east point, with only sea behind it, so it no longer hides
  the streets and gardens north of the old central site.
- Its gated grounds: a gatehouse and a barrier arm that lifts for your car at a crawl, a
  porte-cochère over a fountain turning circle, residents' bays, a formal garden, a
  tennis court, an infinity-pool deck and a private cove (loungers, cabanas, a teak jetty
  with the residents' tender) walled off from the public beach by a granite groyne.
  Security, doormen, a valet and a beach attendant; the guard politely turns strangers away.
- THE SOVEREIGN is retired (kept as a reserve design). Its block is now Sovereign Square
  (boutiques round a garden square) and Monarch One's old block is Regent Court (the bank,
  town houses, a garden court and MONARCH COACHWORKS).
- A respray and repair garage on every island: MONARCH COACHWORKS (Monarch Isle),
  OCEANVIEW AUTO BODY, CORAL COAST GARAGE (Palmshore), PIER GARAGE (Sunset Pier) and the
  CAUSEWAY GARAGE outside Fort Sentinel; same prices, police rule, map badge and GPS.
- Internals: monarch-one.js / monarch-one3d.js, garages-shops.js (`GARAGE_ISLANDS`),
  garage3d-styles.js (coachworks, seaside, lot slabs); `monarch()` reports `monarchOne`
  and `reserveTowers`, `garage()` each shop's island and style. Test garages-islands.mjs.
