# Car cabins: heads under the roof, a new Chevette Z06, badges on every tail
- No head comes through a roof or a window any more (the Chevette Z06 and the other low supercars, the Prestige
  Collection, the police cars): every seat is fitted round the head the game draws, for the tallest man and woman, as
  real cars do it: a little lower and laid back in saloons, low and well back in mid-engined supercars. Drivers lie
  back against their seats with their faces level, and the drive-by pose sits in the same seat.
- The Chevette Z06 is rebuilt after the C8 Z06: thin angular LED lamps over a wide mouth and corner intakes, the big
  scoop behind each door, intakes on the hips, a black roof panel, a squarer tail with slim stacked LED lamps over a
  black fascia with corner vents, four pipes in a square in the middle and an integrated ducktail; the roof's crown
  sits over the driver. The tall wing moved to the Z06 Carbon Aero.
- Every car carries its name on the tail in its maker's lettering (CHEVETTE Z06, REGENT, RANGER, WORKHORSE...), so
  the chase camera tells you what you are driving: letters in the trim atlas, merged into each car's trim (no new
  draw call, texture or material).
- Console: `cabinHeadroom()`; `carModels()` cabins report the real head room and each model's `badge`.
- Tests: cabin-headroom, rear-badges (rendered page; null checks in the suite).
