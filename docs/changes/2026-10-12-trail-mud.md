# Trail mud: real textures, lit spray, ruts and mud on bodies

- The mud a 4x4 throws on the Ridgeline trails is rebuilt from real CC0 textures (Poly Haven's Brown Mud 03, Kenney's
  Particle Pack): rounded, textured clods that smear along their flight and shine when wet, sprite clusters of spray
  lit through their own relief and streaked at speed, fine streaked drops, and mist that lingers in the chase view.
- Tyre ruts have a basin, raised lips and tread lugs; splats are lumpy and lit; both keep the ground's own light and
  shadow and turn glossy when wet. A ford's spray uses the same sprites, in water.
- Mud on vehicles is the mud photo thrown up from the wheels: a ragged line, streaks and flecks above it, with relief.
- Per-tier caps (`MUD_TIER`): LOW throws less than before; the chase view skips what would not show.
- Internals: assets/trail-mud.webp (tools/trail_mud.py); drops and mist go to the effect pool; mud randomness is its own
  stream (`mudRandom`); the club flag and course gates moved to offroad3d-course.js. Console `trailMud(seconds, keys)`
  stages a mud scene without drawing; `offroad().effects` reports the new layers. Test tools/tests/trail-mud.mjs.
