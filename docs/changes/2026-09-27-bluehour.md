# The Blue Hour: poison or a gunfight, a new terrace and a grand entrance

- Mission 2 has two ways to kill Vescari: spike his reserved glass unseen, or draw and fight the whole detail. The silent takedown (and its prompt and hints) is gone.
- The rooftop is refurnished in one luxury look: teak deck, travertine and brass, frameless glass balustrade, marble bar with lit back shelves and velvet stools, tufted velvet lounges, clipped planters, palms, sun loungers, lanterns and evening wear on the guests.
- Vescari's VIP table: linen to the floor, china and crystal place settings, roses, hurricane candles, champagne on ice, velvet chairs; the reserved glass sits on a gold coaster with a pulsing gold ring while it is the target.
- The hotel's entrance: the bus stop, news boxes, bins and meters at the door are gone; a stone portal with a revolving door, a glass canopy ringed with bulbs and lit lettering, a red carpet, topiary, brass rope posts and bollards, lantern standards, a valet stand, two doormen and a valet, and two limousines at the kerb (ordinary parked cars: they can be stolen).
- The terrace is now batched (far fewer draw calls). New console method `blueHourEntrance()`; `roofStealth()` reports Vescari (`boss`). New test mission2-paths.
