# The lockpick and trunks
- A LOCKPICK (a hook pick and a tension wrench) joins the arsenal as a TOOL once it is given (key 9, Settings ·
  Controls; it is saved). It fires nothing, and nobody on the street is afraid of it. It works on foot only.
- With it in hand at a car whose trunk holds something, hold the action key: the player steps to the bumper, kneels
  with both hands at the lock and sets five pins over about five seconds (a soft click per pin, the latch, then the
  lid swings up on its springs over a dark trunk well). Releasing or walking off pauses it; the work is kept for 10 s.
  Without the lockpick in hand the prompt says EQUIP THE LOCKPICK.
- A locked car's driver's door gives in about 3.5 s the same way (three pins), instead of breaking the window; the
  carjack then goes as before.
- Picking a lock is a small theft per pin: with no stars it only counts when the police see it or a passer-by who
  watched phones 911.
- A crash-sprung boot lid now lies along the car's rear deck line (it used to sit at the hood's hinge height).
- Internals: `LOCKPICK_INDEX` 8 (arsenal.js; not in `weapons`), `giveLockpick` / `lockpickOwned` / `lockpickEquipped`;
  vehicle-trunk.js (`c.trunkLoot`, `trunkOpen`, `trunkPoint`, `trunkPickProgress`, `trunkPicking`, `openTrunk`);
  console `lockpick()`, `trunkReport()`, `trunkTarget()`, `lockedDoorTarget()`; tools/tests/lockpick.mjs.
