# Coherent motorcycle spills and recovery

- Lowsides slide onto the bike's falling side; loopouts retain the raised front's backwards release, and over-the-bars crashes keep their forward rotation.
- Riders protect their head, gather their knees, and rise through a supported crouch without snapping upright. Player and traffic riders share the motion and original injury/KO waits.
- Added bounded `riderFallScene` / `riderFallAdvance` console staging and a production solver/pose/root regression across frame rates and adult heights.
