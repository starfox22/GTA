# Bloodier gunfights: blood by calibre and range, point-blank dismemberment

- Every gunshot lets out blood by calibre, range and where it hit: a fine entry puff, a heavier exit spray for
  rifles and heavy guns, a dense burst of blood and tissue from a point-blank shotgun load, a .50 or a close blast.
- The spray marks a wall or a parked car it actually reaches (splashes with drips running down the wall; a stain
  on the car's bodywork); drops landing in a body's pool join it; landed drops are centimetres across, not blots.
- A point-blank shotgun (within ~3 m, nothing past 7 m), a .50 or 25 mm round or a close blast can take a limb
  (forearm, arm, lower leg, leg) or the head: a ragged stump with flesh and bone, the limb thrown and coming to
  rest, arterial spurts for a few seconds, bone chips and a dark mist. A head or a destroyed chest kills; a lost
  limb drops a survivor who bleeds out in 7-13 s. Medics never revive the maimed. The player loses parts only
  when a blast kills him.
- Wounds soak the clothes (or skin) round the entry and, when the round went through, the exit: crowd, police,
  gangs, soldiers and the player's own body. Bodies pool more (1.75 m across for one round, more for the maimed).
- Settings · Gameplay · Gore: FULL (default) or REDUCED (nothing comes off, about half the blood).
- Internals: gore.js (goreHit, goreSever, goreWound, stumps, bleeding out), gore-props.js (severed pieces),
  crowd3d-gore.js (stumps, pieces, the `crowdWound` attribute), blood3d.js (all blood decals in one instanced draw,
  was up to 240 meshes), `strikePerson(..., detail)`, `bleed(..., hit)`, `addCarStain(..., sev)`; blood and gore use
  their own random stream (goreRandom). Console: goreReport, goreShot, goreWallShot, goreCarShot, goreBlast,
  goreSeed, bloodPlanTable.
