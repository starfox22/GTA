# Night headlights, steadier textures and a richer daylight look

- Headlights at night: the player's car and the nearest cars (1 on LOW, 4 MEDIUM, 8 HIGH, 12 ULTRA) now light the world with a real low-beam pattern (hot spot, sharp cut-off rising on the kerb side, long throw along the pavement); kerbs, walls, parked cars and pedestrians in front are lit on the faces that face the car, wet tarmac and paint catch the glare. People and cars standing in the player's beams cast shadows along them. Other traffic lays the same beam shape on the road.
- Lamp glows brighten into a bloom glow (and a faint flare streak on HIGH/ULTRA) when they face the camera; brake lights flare brighter and wash the road redder; reversing cars show white lamps and a white pool behind them; rain streaks glitter in the beams and a soft haze hangs in them in rain and mist (HIGH/ULTRA).
- The player's headlight no longer shines by day (the old spotlight was always on).
- Texture shimmer on HIGH/ULTRA: the street camera is locked to whole pixels (static scenery no longer crawls while the view scrolls), FXAA now runs after the tone curve on every tier (MSAA left bright edges, leaf cut-outs and glints stepped and flickering), the ground's bump no longer flips bright/dark fringes along markings and kerbs as the view moves, and the half-resolution ambient occlusion is upsampled depth-aware (no shade fringes round cars, people and roof edges).
- Daylight: warmer sun and a cooler, deeper sky fill, cool shade and warm highlights, vibrance instead of flat saturation, bloom only on glints by day (the pale paving no longer hangs a milky veil), ambient occlusion kept on sunlit ground.
- Internals: lighting3d-cutaway.js split into cutaway / vehicle-lights / look; CAR LAMPS uniform arrays (`cityCarLampA/B`, `cityCarLampCount`) in `CITY_LIGHT_PARS`; `queueVehicleHalo(sprite, opacity, facing, gain, tint, size)`; quality tiers gain `carLamps`.
- New console methods: `headlights()` (vehicle lights this frame), `lookSwitches(options)` (A/B switches: pixelLock, fxaa, vibrance, carLamps, groundSlopeCap).
