# Chase view draw distance and detail
- The chase view draws far less at the same draw distance (HIGH, camera + shadow passes): at the start
  spot ~1,650 calls and 3.0 M triangles became ~1,200 and 1.8 M; driving an avenue at ~100 km/h ~1,930 and
  3.0 M became ~1,060 and 1.85 M. The street and flight views are unchanged.
- Beyond 175-275 m by tier, a 1024-unit cell is drawn from the far copy of the city (same shapes, facades
  keep their own window light at night); the air keeps the far copy as before.
- The sun's shadow pass leaves out everything that cannot shade what the camera sees, and near cells past
  ~70 m (HIGH) cast from the far copy.
- Small props, breakable furniture and outlying instanced pools step out by their own size and distance;
  people step down by their own distance (street body set past ~26 m, far figures past ~185 m).
- Console: `chaseCamera().view` adds far cells, shadow casters left out, props hidden and `draws` (camera
  and shadow calls and triangles by kind and distance band).
