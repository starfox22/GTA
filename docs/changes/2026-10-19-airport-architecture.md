# Rebuild the airport terminals and aprons

- Southport and Oceanview gain reflective terminal facades, clerestory roofs, hangar doors, detailed control towers, entrance canopies, apron joints and numbered aircraft stands.
- Oceanview's boarding corridors become elevated glass jetbridges with angled docking arms and accordion seals; narrow supports and overhead volumes share the rendered plan for truthful ground/aircraft collision, sight and cover.
- The public airport road keeps 5 m headroom beneath those bridges; sloped walkways descend to the original aircraft doors and all support feet stand outside the carriageway. Full-body sedan/bus/truck/ambulance road sweeps check both lanes.
- Southport's trainer apron is paved consistently on the maps and in 3D, within the existing shoreline. Original parking, taxi routes, runway dimensions and mission locations remain.
- Shared static materials/cell batching retain broad airport silhouettes in distant scenery. A real-Three.js host check verifies bridge/canopy geometry, height-sensitive cover and collision registration.
