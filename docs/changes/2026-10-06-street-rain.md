# Rain and night at street level
- Chase view in the rain: a dense near field of short streaks round the camera, falling at a side view's speed and slanting with the wind, lit by street lamps, neon and headlights; no drop crosses the lens; splashes gather on the street ahead, half of them standing up as small crowns.
- Rain haze in the chase view: the near street stays clear and contrasty while the distance closes in darker and denser (the haze and the sky over it darken with the rain, most at night) instead of a pale lilac veil over everything.
- Wet streets at eye level (HIGH, ULTRA): reflections reach down the street (buildings, lamps, signs, tail lights), mirror more as the view grazes the road, fade at the frame's edges and show the sky's own colour in puddles; less sparkle on damp tarmac. On every tier the lamp and neon streaks on the wet road run towards the camera.
- Night at eye level: lamp halos and vehicle lamp glows no longer swell into blobs near the camera; oncoming head lamps dazzle with a soft glare; the spray behind cars glows red in their tail lights (brighter braking); facades take light from the street lamps in front of them, so side-street walls are no longer pitch black.
- The street view keeps its rain, haze and reflections as before.
- Internals: weather3d-chase.js (near rain, side-view numbers, CHASE RAIN AIR), postfx3d.js SSR `uStreet`, surfaces3d.js radial sheen, signage3d.js `uStreakView` and GLOW_STREET_ANGLE, render3d-streetprops.js street-level halos, lighting3d-sky.js CITY WALL LIGHT; docs/areas/rendering-weather.md (split from rendering.md).
