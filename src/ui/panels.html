THE STREET MANUAL

KNOW THE CONTROLS.

TOUCH Move with the left stick, aim and fire with the right. Context buttons handle driving, flying and missions. Turn them on or off in Settings · Controls.

Press E at the payphone, then Enter to accept the caller’s job. Eleven connected jobs take you across Northbank, Palm Keys and Southport Airport. Explore the wider county via five new crossings: Ridgeline mountain roads, Stonecreek, Northridge, Eastgate, Oceanview and Palmshore. Oceanview International sits in the southern outskirts. Fort Sentinel is a restricted military island in the southeast: E breaches the barrier; steal a tank and use W/S to drive, A/D to pivot and F to fire its cannon. LIFT marks The Blue Hour: press E to ride to its walkable rooftop terrace, bar and return elevator. During Mission 2, collect guest clothes, walk calmly, and hold E near the unaware target for a quiet takedown. Or press P beside his reserved drink on the west table. The drink takes 8 seconds; you can leave while waiting. F draws your concealed pistol. Escape via the elevator. Outside this job, weapons stay holstered on the terrace. The yellow jet ski waits at Bay Launch, marked JET on the Palm Keys shore. Rust jackets belong to Harbor Kings; violet to Glasshouse Crew. Buy weapons at GUN. Sleep at ZZ to heal and advance time. Hospitals provide treatment; bars serve food; clubs open at 20:00. Boats are tied to the wooden river docks. Break police sight and keep hidden until the countdown ends to clear all stars. Steal helicopters at the H markers: Police HQ and Riverside. Slow down over clear ground to land; press E after touchdown. Cars dent and smoke as damage builds. Face your target to shoot. Nearby threats in front of you are aimed at automatically. Mouse aiming is optional. Follow yellow markers for jobs. Green crosses heal; purple crates refill ammo. Four R markers show drive-in respray shops. Drive fully inside an open bay, stop, and press E. Repairs and paint cost $250. Mission 1: leaving the harbor starts a visible 10-second police countdown. Vinny covers the loaded truck’s first respray; use any R garage to lose the cops, then deliver the crates. Mission 10: land, defeat the guards, free Daniel and escort him to the helicopter. Mission 11: fly Daniel and the ledger; V changes landing destination after the manifest is exposed. Escape in the van and drive inside Vinny’s warehouse. Flyable courier planes, private jets and a large airliner wait at the airports. The larger airliner needs Oceanview’s long runway. Hold W to accelerate, then ↑ (climb) to rotate; a notch of flaps (X extends, Z retracts) shortens the take-off roll, and L raises the gear once airborne. Excessive nose-up pitch or too little speed causes a stall (the HUD and a horn warn first): release ↑, lower the nose with ↓ (descend), add W throttle. Lower the gear (L) before landing. On approach keep a little power, reduce descent and briefly raise the nose just before touchdown. Pinch the game view or scroll to zoom out over the city; +/− zoom and 0 resets the view. Only one police helicopter follows you. Hide in the U railway underpass in Northbank, take a boat beneath a bridge, or break sight behind towers and mountain ridges. Stay unseen for 18 seconds and air support leaves. Its search follows your last known position; coming back into view resets the search. Shoot it down with rifles or a bazooka on foot, or use your handgun from a vehicle. A destroyed helicopter cannot be replaced for 45 seconds. Ground patrols may still pursue you. Vehicle gunfire is handgun-only; tank armor blocks ordinary bullets. Find CAS on Palm Keys for roulette. Map: C or FIND ME centers on you. Tap or click any destination for a cyan road route; drag to pan, pinch or use +/− to zoom, and CLEAR ROUTE to remove it. City rail: look for M on the map. Press E at a station to choose any other station; E on the train requests the next stop. Riding a cab or a train (or the Meridian Star at sea), Y skips the ride: the screen fades, the clock moves on as far as the ride would have taken, and you arrive; a cab charges its full fare, and on a train U picks a different stop to skip to. Rail viaducts and station platforms provide real overhead cover. Central Commons has a walking circuit and a separate green cycle loop. Free bicycles wait by park racks and mountain trailheads: hold W to pedal, S brakes, A/D steers. Deer and rabbits flee noise; foxes can approach quietly. Bears warn before charging—back away or reach a vehicle. Wanted levels build gradually between stars; air support takes at least eight seconds to arrive before switching on its searchlight. Mountains: take a Ranger 4×4 from either trailhead to climb Mount Ascent or Needle Ridge. Road tires lose grip on steep slopes. Guns and lodging are available on every island. J jumps from an aircraft when safely above the ground; Space opens the parachute, with automatic opening near the ground. Every key can be changed in Settings · Controls; this manual names the defaults.
