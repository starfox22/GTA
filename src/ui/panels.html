THE STREET MANUAL

KNOW THE CONTROLS.

TOUCH Move with the left stick, aim and fire with the right. Context buttons handle driving, flying and missions. Turn them on or off in Settings · Controls.

THE JOB

A yellow payphone rings for work: walk up, press E, then Enter to accept. Each job opens once you finish the one before. Yellow markers and the arrow lead the way. Everything else in the county is yours between jobs.

THE LAW

Police act on what they see, hear or are told. A crime an officer, a patrol car or the helicopter witnesses brings them at once; a civilian who saw it runs, then phones 911, and the first star comes when that call ends. Units drive in from the nearest station and search where you were reported. Break their line of sight and stay hidden until the countdown ends, or drive into a garage (R on the map, one on every island): a respray clears the stars if no unit saw you drive in. One police helicopter at most: hide under rail viaducts, the underpass or a bridge.

ON THE STREET

You run by default; hold Shift to walk. E beside a car pulls the driver out, and some put up a fight. Face your target to shoot; nearby threats in front of you are aimed at automatically. Firing while backing off keeps you facing the threat. Green crosses heal, purple crates refill ammunition, GUN shops sell weapons, and hospitals patch you up. Falls are real: a cliff or a long drop is fatal on foot or at the wheel.

THE CITY

Tab opens the county map: click any destination for a road route. Cabs, the city rail (M) and the Meridian Star carry you; Y skips the ride. Free bicycles wait by park racks and trailheads. North Point Key's tower lifts (E at the lobby doors) reach the CIRRUS sky bar and a rooftop helipad. The casino, Marea Beach Club, the Sunset Pier rides, the stadium, MONARCH MOTORS and the Ridgeline 4×4 club are open all day or by their hours.

IN THE AIR

Planes: hold W for power, ↑ to rotate, X/Z for flaps and L for the gear. Too much nose-up or too little speed stalls: release ↑, lower the nose, add power. Helicopters land when you slow over clear ground or a flat roof; press E after touchdown. J bails out; press J again to open the parachute. It never opens by itself and needs about 55 m to slow you: the freefall cue turns red when it is too late.

Scroll or +/− to zoom the street camera, 0 to reset. Every key can be changed in Settings · Controls; this manual names the defaults.
